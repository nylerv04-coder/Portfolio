Project Objective

Designed and analyzed a high-speed projectile mechanism for the VEX Robotics "Pushback" competition. The primary engineering challenge required determining the maximum entry velocity of a VEX Octoball into a designated tube without it bouncing out.

Scoring Mechanism

The scoring system required precise intake geometry and outtake sequencing to reliably deliver balls into the elevated goal at high speed. The mechanism was iterated through multiple physical builds alongside simulation refinement.

Simulation & Analysis

To optimize the scoring mechanism, I utilized Ansys Explicit Dynamics to simulate the impact physics. This involved modeling the material properties of the Octoball and the rigid tube structure to accurately predict the coefficient of restitution and energy dissipation upon entry.

Results

The simulation data established a precise threshold for our launcher's motor speeds, ensuring maximum rapid-fire capability while maintaining a 100% retention rate in the target zone.
